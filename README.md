### 🛠️ What I'm Building

#### ♿ Inclusive University System
A multi-university academic platform concept focused on learning resources, course organization, student progress, and accessibility. The initial scope is being shaped around a university workflow.

#### 🚗 RoadSense
A vehicle-focused safety and mobility project combining a phone app, vehicle hardware, and a dashboard-device concept. The project is currently in development; hardware assembly and the user interface are still part of the work ahead.

#### 🧠 Skill Mirror
An AI chatbot concept for my AI coursework, exploring how a focused assistant can help people reflect on and develop their skills.

#### 💚 Touch of Hope
A digital platform for a community-based organization supporting orphans and children experiencing homelessness. I'm working on its web application and role-based workflows.

#### ⛽ FineGas / RayDun
A gas-cylinder wholesale and distribution management concept covering customer and admin workflows, cylinder sizes, payments, and account tracking.

#### 📊 Data Science & Analytics
My data-science project work is currently **paused**. I'll update this section as I resume and make measurable progress.
